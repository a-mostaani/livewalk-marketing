Follow the city past the obvious.

For local guides

  • Set up your Guide access
  • Review incoming walk requests
  • Accept or decline with a clear handoff

The current LivelyWalk flow

Designed to make the next step feel natural.

LivelyWalk’s MVP focuses on the human handoff: a Traveler starts with an idea, a Guide responds, and both enter a shared session when the booking is confirmed.

  1. 01

    Begin from your perspective

    Choose the Traveler or Guide app and sign in to the flow built for you.

  2. 02

    Make or answer a request

    Travelers create a walk request. Guides can review it and accept or decline.

  3. 03

    Step into the session

    A confirmed booking opens the current shared walking-session flow for both sides.
